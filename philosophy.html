Our philosophy

Worthy of rewards.

Medeful comes from meed, an old English word for a reward that has been earned. It is the idea our whole group is built around.

Care works best when the people giving it and the people receiving it are on the same side.

Healthcare businesses often make a choice: build around the provider, or build around the patient.

We think that choice is a false one. A practice that is well run, well marketed and well supported gives better care. Patients who feel looked after stay, return and refer. Each side helps the other grow.

So everything Medeful does is measured against one question: does this reward both?

The partnership
