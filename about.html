About us

A group built by people who know dental.

Medeful brings together established businesses that have each spent years helping practices grow. Now they work as one.

Our story

Why we came together.

Each of our businesses was solving one part of the same problem for the same practice owners.

Marketing brought the patients in. Technology kept track of them. Consulting improved how the practice ran. Practice sales helped owners move on or step up. Working separately meant clients had to join the dots themselves.

Medeful joins those dots. One group, one platform and one shared belief that providers and patients should be rewarded together.
